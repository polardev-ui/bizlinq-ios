Terms of Service

Effective date: October 2026

The basics

BizLinq is a directory of business networking meetings. Browsing is free. Posting a meeting requires a poster subscription ($10/month or $99/year, sold by Apple through In-App Purchase) with unlimited live posts — and you can edit any live listing anytime. Attending meetings may have fees set by the host.

Your content

You own the meetings you post. By posting, you give us permission to display that content to other users. You promise your listings are accurate, lawful, and yours to share. No spam, scams, adult content, hate, or harassment — zero tolerance.

Reporting and moderation

Every meeting can be reported from its detail screen, and any host can be blocked so their meetings disappear from your view. We review reports and remove violating content and repeat offenders. Users who believe their content was removed in error can appeal at support@bizlinq.app.

Subscriptions and refunds

Subscriptions start with a 14-day free trial (one per account), then renew automatically at $10/month or $99/year until cancelled (manage or cancel anytime in iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions). All billing, renewals, and refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's terms.

Acceptable use

One account per person. Don't scrape the directory, don't resell listings, don't post misleading events, and don't use BizLinq for anything illegal. We may suspend accounts that break these rules.

Disclaimers

Meetings are hosted by independent third parties, not by us. We don't vet hosts, venues, or events — attend at your own judgment. BizLinq is provided "as is" without warranties, to the maximum extent the law allows.
