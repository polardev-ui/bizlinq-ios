Support

We're here to help.

Contact us

Email support@bizlinq.app and we'll get back to you within two business days. Include your account display name and, if it's about a meeting, the meeting title and city.

Common fixes

  • No meetings near me? Widen your radius or try Nationwide — new groups join every week. Virtual events always show regardless of radius.
  • Can't post? Posting needs two things: a signed-in account and an active poster plan (Post tab → See poster plans). There are no posting limits — publish as often as you like.
  • Purchase not showing? Tap "Restore purchase" on the paywall, and make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.
  • Wrong location? Update your ZIP and radius anytime in Profile → Search area.

Report a meeting or host

Open any meeting → tap the ••• menu → Report meeting. We review every report and remove spam, scams, and inappropriate content. To stop seeing someone entirely, use Block host in the same menu.

Delete your account

  1. Cancel any live meetings from My Posts (optional — deletion removes them anyway).
  2. Email support@bizlinq.app from your account email with the subject "Delete my BizLinq account".
  3. We confirm and delete your account, posts, RSVPs, and profile within 30 days.

Note: App Store subscriptions are separate — cancel yours in iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
